Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

How does WhisperAI stay completely hidden during screen shares?

WhisperAI utilizes the Windows API SetWindowDisplayAffinity(hwnd, WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE). This signals the Windows Desktop Window Manager (DWM) composition engine to exclude the WhisperAI window from all desktop capture streams, including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and OBS Studio.

How does audio capture work without a bot?

WhisperAI leverages native Windows WASAPI Loopback capture to read audio directly from your default audio output device (what you hear through your headphones/speakers) in parallel with your local microphone. No audio routing drivers or virtual bots are ever needed.

What platforms are supported?

The Windows desktop application (Windows 10 & 11 64-bit) is the primary focus and available today. macOS, Android, and iOS companion applications are next on our roadmap following the Windows release.

Are my questions or conversations logged?

No. WhisperAI follows a strict zero-retention policy. You provide your own API key (Groq, Anthropic, OpenAI) or connect to a local Ollama instance on localhost:11434. Requests flow directly between your machine and your chosen LLM endpoint.
