        Details
        
      Questions
      
      
      
      
      
        
      
          
      
            
      How is it hidden during a screen share?
      
            
          
      
          
      
            On Windows the window calls SetWindowDisplayAffinity(hwnd, WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE),
            which tells the Desktop Window Manager to leave it out of every capture stream — Zoom, Meet, Teams, OBS,
            Windows Game Bar, all of them. macOS uses the equivalent private-window flag.
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      How does it hear the interviewer with no bot?
      
            
          
      
          
      
            WASAPI loopback capture reads whatever is playing out of your speakers or headphones, alongside your mic.
            No virtual audio cable, no bot in the meeting, no "recording" indicator.
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Which models can I use? Do I need a subscription?
      
            
          
      
          
      
            No WhisperAI subscription. Bring a key for Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter (all have free tiers),
            or run a local model with Ollama. The app fetches each provider's live model list, so new models appear
            automatically and retired ones are swapped out for you.
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Is anything I say or ask stored?
      
            
          
      
          
      
            No. There's no WhisperAI account and no server in the loop. Your key lives in the OS keychain, settings
            live in local storage, and prompts go straight from your machine to the model endpoint you chose.
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Which platforms are supported?
      
            
          
      
          
      
            Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit) is the primary target and is ready now. A macOS stealth build ships alongside it;
            the capture-exclusion path is implemented for both.
